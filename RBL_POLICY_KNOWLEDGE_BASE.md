# [Policy 0] Handbook Overview, Governance & Applicability

**Policy:** Handbook Overview, Governance & Legal Scope  
**Section:** Purpose, Scope, Revision Rights & Statutory Precedence  
**Source Page(s):** 2, 3

## Policy / Rule
- **Purpose:** The Associate Handbook is designed for easy reference for store Associates on the rolls of Reliance Brands Group Companies. It provides an understanding of Company Policies, Processes, culture, and social security benefits, ensuring associates are well-informed and empowered.
- **Scope of Guidance:** The handbook provides guidance and does not replace formal terms of employment or any other company rules and regulations. Associates are required to familiarize themselves with their terms of employment and applicable Reliance Brands code of conduct and policies.
- **Company Revision Rights:** The company reserves the right to interpret, modify, revise, supplement, or rescind any policies or portion of the Associate Handbook from time to time as it deems appropriate. Such revisions may be made at the Company's sole discretion and may be made with or without prior notice.
- **Statutory Conflict & Legal Precedence:** In the event of any conflict between the provisions of applicable law and those outlined in this handbook, the provisions of applicable law shall take precedence.

## Eligibility / Applicability
- **Applicable Cadres:** Applicable to store Associates on the rolls of Reliance Brands Group Companies who belong to **Grade L, P & T**.
- **Apprentices:** For Apprentices, **only the Leave Policy and GPA (Group Personal Accident) Insurance** are applicable.

## Employee Responsibilities
- Familiarize themselves with their specific terms of employment, company rules, policies, and the Reliance Brands Code of Conduct.
- Regularly check the **PeopleFirst** digital platform or get in touch with Human Resources (HR) to stay updated on revised policies.

## Process / Procedure
- Associates seeking updated policy documents or clarifications should access the **PeopleFirst** digital platform or consult their HR representative.

## Source Reference
- Associate Handbook, Page 2 and Page 3.

## 1.7 Dress Code: Uniform Standards, Allocation, Return & Reimbursement

**Policy:** General Information  
**Section:** 1.7 Dress Code (Item 1: Uniform Standards)  
**Source Page(s):** 6, 7

### Policy / Rule
- **Standard:** Every Associate is expected to appear and dress appropriately so the professional image contributes to an impression of an esteemed organization. Dress Code defined by the Brand must be worn at all times while on duty.
- **Uniform Standards:** Associates are required to wear the designated company/brand uniform provided to them during working hours. The uniform must be clean, well-maintained, and worn in its entirety.
- **Purpose:** Outlines the issuance and usage of uniforms applicable to employees, in line with Brand guidelines, ensuring role-based uniform allocation, accountability, and fair recovery practices.
- **Uniform Allocation:** Issued based on the employee's role and designation, aligned with Brand standards and requirements.
- **Uniform Cost:** Determined as per Brand policy and may vary based on role, category, and season.
- **Invoicing Standards for Reimbursement:** All uniform bills must clearly mention GST details and carry a proper stamp, irrespective of whether uniforms are procured from local tailors or branded vendors. Bills without GST information and stamp will not be accepted.

### Eligibility / Applicability
- Applicable to store/geography level employees as defined in the Brand Uniform policy. Applies both while working in the store and when out on company assignments.

### Employee Responsibilities
- Wear the complete, clean, and well-maintained brand uniform at all times while on duty.
- Sign a **Uniform Issuance Acknowledgement Form** upon receiving the uniform, confirming receipt and awareness of applicable costs.
- Keep uniforms clean and in good condition.
- Report lost or stolen uniforms immediately.
- Report stained, ripped, or damaged uniforms immediately to the Store Manager for replacement based on defect where applicable as per Brand policy.
- Return uniforms in clean condition to the Store Manager or designated personnel on the last working day upon exit.
- Reach out to HRBPs to understand Brand uniform entitlements and brand-level policies in detail.

### Manager / Supervisor Responsibilities
- Store Manager must replace stained, ripped, or damaged uniforms based on defect where applicable per Brand policy.
- For uniform reimbursement tab activation on PeopleFirst, the Store Manager must email an Excel file of the concerned employee to the HRBP with required column formatting.
- Store Manager receives clean uniforms on employee's last working day and issues No Dues Certificate (NDC).

### Prohibited / Restricted Actions
- Employees are strictly prohibited from wearing the uniform beyond work-related purposes, such as for personal use.
- Submitting reimbursement bills without valid GST details and proper vendor stamps.

### Process / Procedure
- **PeopleFirst Reimbursement Tab Activation Workflow:**
  1. Store Manager shares an Excel file of the concerned employee with the HRBP attached via email.
  2. Excel file must contain the following columns:
     - Column 1: Employee Number
     - Column 2: Bill From Date (Format strictly `dd.mm.yyyy`)
     - Column 3: Bill To Date (Format strictly `dd.mm.yyyy`)
     - Column 4: Valid From Date (Format strictly `dd.mm.yyyy`)
     - Column 5: Valid To Date (Format strictly `dd.mm.yyyy`)
     - Column 6: Eligible Amount (Entered as numbers only, e.g., `40000`)
  3. **Turnaround Time (TAT):** Tab activation takes **24–48 working hours** from receipt of complete and correct details.
  4. Employee submits GST-compliant, stamped bills through PeopleFirst for reimbursement processing.

### Consequences / Disciplinary Action
- Replacement costs for lost or stolen uniforms will be deducted from payroll as per brand guidelines.
- **Recovery Applicability:** Recovery of uniform cost is applicable in any of the following scenarios (as defined in Brand level Policy):
  - Exit before completion of the minimum service period (0 to 12 months), and/or
  - Exit before completion of the season period (0 to 6 months), and/or
  - Loss or damage of uniform due to negligence or misuse, and/or
  - Failure to return the uniform at the time of exit.
- Failure to return uniforms in clean condition on the last working day may result in a deduction from the final paycheck / Full & Final settlement.
- HR Ops recovers uniform cost only upon receiving the NDC with the recovery value communicated to the employee.

### Source Reference
- Associate Handbook, Section 1.7 (Item 1), Page 6–7. (See Annexure for brand-specific recovery matrices).

# [Policy 2] Payment of Salary & Deductions

**Policy:** Payment of Salary & Deductions  
**Section:** 2. Payment of Salary & Deductions  
**Source Page(s):** 8

## Policy / Rule
- **Salary Payment Schedule:** For all Associates on RBL Brand roles, salary disbursal is on the **last working day of the month**.
- **Authorized Deductions:** Authorized deductions from associate's salaries will be made in accordance with the *Payment of Wages Act, 1936*, and its relevant rules. These deductions include but are not limited to statutory contributions, taxes, and other legally permissible deductions.
- **Additional Deductions / Recovery for Losses:** In compliance with applicable laws, the company reserves the right to make deductions from an associate's salary for reasons including, but not limited to:
  - Cash shortages
  - Inventory discrepancies
  - Losses resulting from associate negligence during the course of their duties.
- **Due Process Requirement:** Such deductions will be made following a thorough investigation, and the deduction shall not be made until the employed person has been given an opportunity of showing cause against the deduction.
- **Post-Separation Legal Recovery:** If the associate is separated or the amount is not recoverable from the salary or full and final settlement, the company may initiate appropriate legal proceedings to recover the amount.

## Employee Responsibilities
- Associates are expected to cooperate fully during investigations related to any salary deductions.

## Manager / Supervisor Responsibilities
- Conduct thorough investigations into discrepancies and ensure show-cause opportunities are provided before authorizing recovery deductions.

## Process / Procedure
- Incident / discrepancy identified  ->  Thorough investigation conducted  ->  Associate given opportunity of showing cause  ->  Authorized deduction executed from payroll or Full & Final settlement  ->  If unrecovered post-separation, legal proceedings initiated.
- **Dispute Resolution:** Any disputes regarding deductions should be brought to the attention of the HR department for resolution.

## Consequences / Disciplinary Action
- Payroll deduction, recovery from Full & Final settlement, and potential civil or criminal legal proceedings for unrecovered losses resulting from negligence or misconduct.

## Source Reference
- Associate Handbook, Section 2, Page 8.

## 4.4 Maternity, Adoption & Miscarriage Leave

**Policy:** Attendance and Leave  
**Section:** Maternity Leave  
**Source Page(s):** 10

### Policy / Rule
- **Statutory Coverage:** Associates who do not qualify for maternity leave under ESIC will be covered under the *Maternity Benefit Act*, provided they have worked for at least **80 days in the 12 months** before their expected delivery date.
- **Maternity Leave Duration:**
  - a. Not exceeding **182 calendar days (26 weeks)** on full pay for up to two surviving children.
  - b. Leave not exceeding **84 calendar days (12 weeks)** from third child onwards.
- **Commissioning Mother:** Will be provided **12 weeks** leave.
- **Miscarriage / Medical Termination Leave:** As per Maternity Benefit Act, **6 weeks (not exceeding 42 calendar days)** will be provided as leave.
- **Child Adoption Leave:**
  - a. Up to **28 calendar days (4 weeks)** on full pay during the first 12 weeks of Child's adoption.
  - b. In case child is **less than 3 months of age**, leave not exceeding **84 calendar days (12 weeks)** on full pay.

### Eligibility / Applicability
- Female associates with at least 80 days of active work in the 12 months preceding expected delivery date (who do not qualify under ESIC).
- *Exclusion:* Not applicable to trainee apprentices.

### Exceptions
- Trainee apprentices are exempt/excluded from Maternity Leave provisions.

- Associate Handbook, Section 4, Page 10.

- **Sickness Benefit Qualifying Conditions:**
  - Every Insured Person (IP) should have paid ESIC contribution for **not less than 78 days** during the corresponding contribution period.

- **Contribution and Benefit Periods:**

| :--- | :--- |

| **October to March** | **July to December** |

| | **Enhanced Sickness Benefit** | Payment of contribution for 78 days in corresponding contribution period | 14 days for Tubectomy and 7 days for Vasectomy, extendable on medical advice | 100% of the average wages |
